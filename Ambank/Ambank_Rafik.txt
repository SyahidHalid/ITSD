1. Fahami Fungsi Credit Risk di EXIM Bank

Secara umum, Credit Risk bertanggungjawab untuk:

Menilai risiko pelanggan sebelum pembiayaan diluluskan
Memantau portfolio pembiayaan sedia ada
Mengukur Probability of Default (PD)
Menentukan Loss Given Default (LGD)
Menilai Expected Credit Loss (ECL)
Mengenalpasti akaun bermasalah (Impaired Account)
Menyediakan laporan kepada Management, BNM dan Board

6. EXIM-Specific Questions

Oleh kerana EXIM ialah DFI yang menyokong eksport negara:

Anda perlu faham:

Trade Financing

Mereka mungkin tanya:

Kenapa risiko kredit EXIM berbeza dengan bank komersial?

Jawapan:

EXIM mempunyai mandat pembangunan (development mandate). Oleh itu ada pembiayaan yang mempunyai impak strategik kepada negara, tetapi profil risikonya mungkin lebih tinggi berbanding portfolio bank komersial biasa.

Contoh Jawapan Interview Yang Lengkap

"When assessing a borrower, I would apply the 5Cs of Credit. First, 
Character to evaluate management integrity and repayment track record. 
Second, Capacity to assess whether the company generates sufficient cash flow to service its debt obligations. 
Third, Capital to understand the financial strength and commitment of shareholders. 
Fourth, Collateral to determine the secondary source of repayment in the event of default. 
Finally, Conditions to evaluate industry, economic, country, and geopolitical risks that may impact the borrower's repayment ability.

LGD = EAD * (1 - recovery rate)
