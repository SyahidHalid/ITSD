Financing Score & Prioritisation Guide

Final Financing Score

Final Financing Score = (Raw Data Score × 0.65) + (ELSA Assessment Score × 0.35). The score is a prioritisation indicator and is not a financing approval.

How the Raw Data Score is calculated (maximum 100 points)
  • Financing Demand (35 points): Market expansion (10), capital investment (10), new product/service (10), and prior financing attempt (5).
  • Growth Potential (25 points): Improving performance (5), product growth (5), industry lifecycle (up to 5), product/service lifecycle (up to 5), and revenue available (5).
  • Financial Readiness (25 points): Bank account (5), audited accounts (5), SST (3), financial lifecycle (up to 6), and financial-system lifecycle (up to 6).
  • Sustainability (15 points): Business operating more than 5 years (5), ACTIVE status (3), good industry performance (3), no late-payment problem (2), and workforce greater than zero (2).

Lifecycle-based points are adjusted using the configured factor: Take-off/Maturity (1.0), Pre-Take-off (0.6), Renewal (0.5), and Stagnation (0.3).

Tier Explanation

High (80–100): strong financing potential indicators. Medium (60–79): moderate financing potential indicators. Emerging and Low indicate progressively developing or lower current indicators; their exact score bands follow the active UDX scoring configuration.

Review Status

Need to review means the record is flagged for human verification before follow-up or decision-making. It is not an automatic rejection or proof of ineligibility.

How to interpret intervention leads

Score Calculation

Intervention Score is the UDX prioritisation score for the relevant intervention type, based on the score and breakdown supplied in the dataset. Use the score breakdown and reasons to understand the contributing factors.

Tier Explanation

Tier groups intervention leads into configured score bands to support prioritisation. The displayed tier follows the UDX scoring configuration; it is not a guarantee of programme acceptance.

Review Status

Review means REVIEW_REQUIRED is flagged and the lead should be checked by a staff member before action. It is a verification flag, not a rejection.
